Case Study · Concept Project

alon: a sea-inspired skincare brand, from logo to Shopify store

Alon means “wave” in Tagalog. I created alon as a self-initiated brief to show the full job I do for small e-commerce brands: a brand identity, the Shopify store that sells it, a launch landing page, the packaging customers unbox, and the social posts that bring them in — all as one consistent system.

Role

Brand & UI Designer

Brand Identity Shopify Store Landing Page Packaging Figma Canva

The problem

Small skincare brands compete for attention with labels that have far bigger budgets. Three things usually hold them back:

  • 01 They look generic. A stock logo and a default theme don't earn the trust needed to buy something that goes on your skin.
  • 02 Every touchpoint looks different. The Instagram feed, the store, and the box arrive looking like three different companies.
  • 03 Design and build are split. A designer hands over a file, a developer interprets it, and the store that launches isn't the one that was designed.

1 · Brand identity

The brand is built around one idea: the calm of the Philippine sea. A circular wave mark sits beside a lowercase serif wordmark, so the logo reads as quiet and premium rather than loud.

Deep Tide#0F3B3A

Sea Mist#CFE3DD

Coral Clay#E07A5F

Sand#F3ECE2

Ink#1C1C1C

Typography

Fraunces for headlines gives the editorial, premium feel of a magazine. DM Sans for body copy stays clean and readable on small product labels and on mobile.

2 · Product range

Three products, each with its own colour from the palette, so customers can tell them apart at a glance while the range still reads as one family.

3 · Shopify store & launch page

Beauty shoppers decide in seconds, and most of them are on a phone. The store and landing page were designed around that.

One message above the fold

The homepage leads with a single line — “Calm skin, inspired by the sea.” — and one primary button. Trust badges (dermatologist tested, reef-safe, cruelty-free, made in the Philippines) sit directly underneath, answering the first questions a skincare buyer has before they scroll.

A mobile product page built to convert

Price, size options, and the Add to Cart button stay in reach of the thumb. Ingredients and reviews come after, for the shoppers who want them.

A single-product launch page

For ad traffic, a dedicated Sea Kelp Serum page tells one story in order: hero → proof → three key ingredients → a three-step ritual → reviews → one call to action. No menu of distractions between the visitor and the checkout.

4 · Packaging, print & social

For an online brand, the unboxing is the first time a customer holds the product. The system carries through to every physical and social touchpoint:

Serum box & labels

A deep-tide box with a thin coral wave line, and a label system shared across all three products.

Business cards & thank-you insert

“Salamat for choosing calm.” — a card in every order that turns a delivery into a moment worth posting.

Stickers

A round “Reef-Safe · Vegan · Cruelty-Free” seal that doubles as a promise and a packaging detail.

Instagram kit

Six reusable post templates — product, quote, tips, lifestyle, texture — so the feed always matches the store.

What a client gets from this

  • ▸ One brand system that works the same on a bottle, a box, a phone screen, and an Instagram grid.
  • ▸ A store and launch page designed around how beauty shoppers actually buy: on mobile, in seconds.
  • ▸ No hand-off gap: I design it in Figma and build it on Shopify myself, so what launches is what was designed.
